# Object Replacer

Replaces **every** object with a given object ID with a selection of your own
objects — spikes, saws, blocks, decorations, anything with an object ID.

Build your custom spike out of whatever objects you like, select them in the
editor, hit **Replace** in the top bar (or press **F5**) and capture the
selection. Every spike in the level is now your custom spike, in any level you
play.

* Replacements follow the original object's position, rotation, scale and flips.
* Groups, color channels and layers are carried over, so triggers still work.
* Works on online levels, saved levels and editor playtests.
* Optionally apply the replacements in the editor too, and save the level to
  bake them in permanently.
